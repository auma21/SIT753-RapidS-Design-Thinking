Specification & Architecture

Prototype Design Rationale

Problem

Students may form project teams without adequate information about complementary skills, role preferences, and development goals.

Design Response

TeamForge AI provides explainable recommendations and team-balance information.

Responsible-AI Principle

“The AI recommends. Humans decide.”

MVP Scope (Active in prototype)

  • Student Profile
  • Project Browser
  • Team Recommendation
  • Explainability
  • Team Balance
  • Alternative Teams
  • Data Transparency
